Kueue:
  - Kueue and its Job integration are required. Every session Job is submitted suspended.
  - Community default routes to ClusterQueue {{ include "skaha.defaultClusterQueue" . }}, which must be labelled canfar.net/community=default.
  - Skaha creates one LocalQueue per user in {{ include "skaha.workloadNamespace" . }} on first launch and never deletes it.

Quick checks:
  - kubectl get deployment {{ .Release.Name }}-skaha-tomcat -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  - kubectl get clusterqueue {{ include "skaha.defaultClusterQueue" . }} --show-labels 2>/dev/null || true
  - kubectl get localqueues -n {{ include "skaha.workloadNamespace" . }} -l app.kubernetes.io/managed-by=skaha 2>/dev/null || true

If user Jobs remain Pending:
- Verify ClusterQueue quotas are sufficient.
- Confirm priority classes referenced by sessions exist or are created.
